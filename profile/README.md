# Retvest

**AI on your own servers.**

Retvest deploys AI on your organisation’s own servers or cloud. Bring your team, company knowledge and chosen models into one environment, with RIL to record supported work.

[Website](https://retvest.com/) · [Retvest Deploy](https://retvest.com/products/deploy/) · [RIL](https://retvest.com/products/ril/) · [Documentation](https://retvest.com/docs/)

[How to deploy AI on your own servers](https://retvest.com/docs/self-hosted-ai/) covers architecture, model hosting, data flow and pilot planning. Use the [editable deployment brief](https://retvest.com/assets/retvest-deployment-planning-template.txt) to prepare your own requirements.

## Retvest Deploy

The business AI environment for infrastructure your organisation operates. Deploy brings together Chat, Work and Code, company knowledge, agents and access controls. Your organisation chooses the model endpoint around its workflows and infrastructure.

RIL, the Retvest Intelligence Layer, is included. It connects supported, enrolled work to its inputs, activity, review decisions and saved outputs.

[Discuss a walkthrough or pilot](https://retvest.com/for-business/). Availability, integrations and operating responsibilities are agreed for each deployment.

## Sidekick

Sidekick by Retvest is a separate, free experimental desktop assistant for individuals. Use voice or text, share context and choose a supported local model or your own provider account. Platform limitations apply; optional providers may charge separately.

[Explore Sidekick](https://retvest.com/sidekick/) · [Downloads and release notes](https://github.com/retvest/sidekick-downloads)
